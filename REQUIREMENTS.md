# Requirements

What this repository must do, what it does beyond that, and what it leaves out on purpose. **Minimum** is what the
interview task asks for: each of those lines must be met, and each one points to the code, scenario or run that proves
it. **Extended** is what the system does beyond the minimum, because the minimum alone would not hold up under a real rush. **Out of scope** is
what was left out on purpose, so a missing feature is a choice and not something that was forgotten.

The behaviour itself is in the Gherkin scenarios in [specs/apps/flash/](specs/apps/flash/README.md). When this page and a
scenario disagree, the scenario wins.

### Out of scope

The reasons, and what would make each one worth adding, are in
[what was left out](README.md#what-was-left-out-and-why).

- Several products, or several sales running at the same time ([decision 010](docs/explanation/decisions/010-one-sale-at-a-time.md)).
- A real payment provider.
- Password reset, an e-mail check and a two-factor step.
- A page for operators. They use the API, and [`api-flash-be.http`](api-flash-be.http) has every call.
- A shopper who gives a hold back before it ends. The hold ends by itself.
- Anything after the purchase: delivery, e-mails, returns.

### Out of scope

- Deploying to a live cloud. Postgres, Redis and nginx run in Docker, and the payment is a mock in the process.
- Failover for Postgres and Redis. Each runs as one instance; the next steps are in
  [how it scales](README.md#how-it-scales-and-where-it-stops).
- Keeping queue places through a Redis restart. A shopper whose place is lost joins again
  ([decision 001](docs/explanation/decisions/001-queue-hold-pay.md)).
- Restarting an API instance that crashed. The containers have no restart policy, which is left to the platform. The
  other instances keep serving, but no test stops an instance.
- Metrics, tracing and alerts. The API writes logs only.
- Speed numbers that hold beyond one laptop. k6 and the servers share its CPUs, so the times are relative; the data
  checks are exact ([what the times mean](docs/explanation/load-test-findings.md#what-the-times-mean)).
- TLS. It is expected in front of the stack, and the cookies are `Secure` there.
